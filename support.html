      Good to know

      • Eviction

        Know your rights

        Lockouts are illegal in NYC. An eviction has to go through a court process, and tenants facing eviction in Housing Court can get free legal help.

        Eviction prevention and tenant rights (HPD) (official NYC page, opens in a new tab)

      • Renting with a voucher

        Source of income discrimination

        If a landlord or broker refuses to rent or show you an apartment because you use government assistance to pay rent, that may be source of income discrimination. The official page explains how to report it.

        Source of income discrimination (official NYC page, opens in a new tab)

      • Not sure where to start?

        Call DSS at 718-557-1399

        The DSS OneNumber can answer questions about rental assistance and homelessness prevention. Or call 311 and ask for the Tenant Helpline.
